Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. ForTran appears as an instrument by AE Cobley.

Two things worth knowing:

  • The Level knob starts 20 dB down. The 2003 original ran hot enough to clip a dense chord, so the output trim defaults to its centre and the top of its travel is exactly the original’s level.
  • The pitch wheel covers a whole octave either way, not two semitones. That is the DXi’s own law, kept deliberately.
